Privacy policy

For the Plainbill iPhone app. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version

Your invoices, estimates and clients stay on your iPhone. There is no account, and we don't collect, see, sell or share any data.

Who we are

Plainbill is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: apricate00@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your business details and logo, your clients, your invoices and estimates, how many invoices you have sent this month, and whether you bought Unlimited. All of it is stored only on your iPhone. The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

When something leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. Tapping Send PDF, Back up or Export spreadsheet opens your iPhone's share sheet, and the file goes wherever you pick: Mail, Messages, Files or another app. From then on that app's own privacy policy applies. Plainbill never sends anything by itself.

Your logo

If you add a logo, the app asks to see your photo library and copies only the one picture you choose. It is kept on your phone and printed on your PDFs.

Purchases

Unlimited is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns from Apple whether you own Unlimited. We never see your name or payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete documents and clients in the app, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to keep or remove.

Children

Plainbill collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Your rights

Questions about privacy: apricate00@gmail.com. In the UK you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
