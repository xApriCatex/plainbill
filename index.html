Invoices and estimates on your iPhone, sent as a PDF. No account, no sign-in, no subscription. Everything stays on your phone.

Common questions

How do I send an invoice? Open it and tap Send PDF. Your iPhone's share sheet opens: pick Mail, Messages, WhatsApp, Files or any other app. The invoice is marked Sent once it has gone.

Free invoices: 3 invoices sent each calendar month are free, and the count starts again on the 1st. Sending the same invoice again does not count twice. Estimates, backups and the spreadsheet export are always free.

Unlimited: one payment removes the monthly limit for good. It is not a subscription. On a new iPhone, open My business and tap Restore purchase.

Turning an estimate into an invoice: open the estimate, tap More, then Turn into invoice. The lines and client are copied; the estimate is kept.

My logo: My business, Add logo. It goes in the top corner of every PDF.

A new iPhone: on the old one, My business, Back up, and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or your email. On the new one, install Plainbill, then My business, Restore, and pick that file.

For my accountant: My business, Export spreadsheet (CSV), gives one row per invoice and estimate with totals.

Invoice numbers: invoices count up from INV-0001 and estimates from EST-0001. A number is never given out twice, even after a restore.
